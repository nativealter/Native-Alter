Mission

We exist at the intersection of what already works and what is only now becoming possible. Our practice is one of iteration — taking the proven foundations of native and conventional software and augmenting them with the leverage of large language models.

This is not about replacement. It is about knowing where intelligence belongs in a system, and placing it there with precision. The result is software that inherits the reliability of established methods while gaining the adaptability that only modern AI can provide.

Sustainability shapes every decision we make — in architecture, in process, and in the long-term thinking that informs what we build.

Renqore

Recruitment automation for
agencies that scale.

Renqore is Native Alter's first product — a recruitment automation platform built for agencies that need to move fast without losing precision. It screens, evaluates, and ranks hundreds of candidates in minutes, handling the workflow so your team can stay focused on what matters: making placements.

Powered by LLM evaluation at its core, Renqore parses CVs automatically, scores every candidate against your defined criteria, and surfaces a ranked shortlist tailored to each client's requirements. No manual sifting. No wasted hours.
